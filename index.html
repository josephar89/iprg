The Heart of Our Community

For over three decades, the IPRG has been a vibrant, supportive community for Indian Catholic priests and religious serving in and around Paris. Though our membership is naturally floating—with priests and religious arriving for higher studies, specialized formation, or short-term pastoral mission—we exist to foster connection, spiritual solidarity, and shared joy.

Our Journey

Humble Beginnings

The story of the IPRG began over three decades ago with a simple desire for connection. When Fr. Balthazar Castelino MEP arrived in Paris from a mission in Madagascar, he proposed a modest idea to Fr. Lawrence Pius Dorairaj (then the Tamil chaplain in Paris, now the Bishop of Dharmapuri): to host a brief afternoon get-together over tea and coffee.

With incredible enthusiasm, Fr. Lawrence reached out to his contacts. Despite the short notice, the response was overwhelming. More than 40 individuals gathered for that inaugural meeting.

The gathering featured a special sharing by Fr. Charles D’Souza (SVD), who was awaiting transit to Africa. He briefed attendees on the latest news from India. Though Fr. Charles later passed away from cancer, his contribution to our first meeting remains a cherished memory.

Establishing a Tradition

That first meeting was held at the historic Missions Étrangères de Paris (MEP), with Fr. Balthasar and Fr. Lawrence personally covering the expenses. The joy was so profound that the group immediately decided it could not be a one-time event.

A democratic structure was formed by electing members to organize events, and the community shifted to a shared-contribution model to cover expenses joyfully.

The IPRG Today

Today, the IPRG has blossomed into a mature, 35-year-old institution. While some founding members have moved to new missions or gone to their eternal rest, the spirit of the IPRG has only grown stronger.

From 40 attendees, our family has expanded to approximately 100 active members, ensuring that Indian priests and religious serving in France always have a place to share their lives, their faith, and their joy.

3rd October 2026

35th Jubilee & Website Launch

To mark the historic 35th year of the IPRG, this official website was launched on 3rd October 2026 under the patronage of our founders, Bp. Lawrence Pius Dorairaj (Bishop of Dharmapuri) and Rev. Fr. Balthazar Castelino MEP.

This digital platform opens a new chapter for our community, connecting Indian priests and religious across France, preserving our history, and fostering fraternal fellowship for generations to come.

Missions Étrangères de Paris (MEP)
Historical Tribute MEP & Madurai Jesuit Mission

Seeds Sown in India, Fruits Witnessed in France

The historic Missions Étrangères de Paris (MEP) holds extraordinary significance in sustaining the IPRG for 35 years. Alongside the legendary Madurai Jesuit Mission, MEP missionaries sowed the early seeds of faith across southern India centuries ago. Today, as Indian priests and religious gather at the MEP headquarters in Paris, we stand in profound gratitude as living witnesses of those fruitful seeds—now serving in France and giving life and meaning to our shared mission.

Message from IPRG President

Fr. Martin MARIA FRANCIS, SDB

President, IPRG

With a feeling of humble gratitude to Almighty God, I feel privileged and proud to be part of this IPRG family. I express my heartfelt gratitude to the Missions Étrangères de Paris (MEP), which welcomes each one of us whenever we come and has always been our mother house; to the beloved founders of our IPRG; to all our former Presidents; to all past and present Coordinators; and to all those who have been part of our IPRG family since 1991, shaping it over the years, as well as to those who are currently part of it, adding flavour and giving life and meaning to it.

Looking back over the 35 years of the history and journey of our IPRG, I cannot but marvel at the simple yet far-sighted vision and optimistic objective of our founders, Most Rev. Lawrence Pius Dorairaj—then a student and Tamil chaplain in Paris, and now the Bishop of Dharmapuri Diocese in Tamil Nadu—and Rev. Fr. Balthazar Castelino, MEP. Little did they know that their initiative would grow into an enduring legacy, welcoming under its mantle priests and religious from different parts of India who come to France either for their studies or for mission. Each one brings with him or her a unique background and identity, shaped by the region of India from which they come and by the particular charism of the Congregation or Diocese to which they belong.

At this juncture, as our IPRG family celebrates its 35th anniversary, I extend my sincere and joyful wishes to all its members. I wish our IPRG family a long and fruitful life, with its enduring spirit and richness being passed on from one generation to another, thus ensuring its continued existence and growth towards its 50th and 100th anniversaries and beyond, and the many years beyond, always remaining faithful to the spirit that inspired our founders.

May the Lord, the source of all wisdom, knowledge, and mission, continue to protect, guide, and bless all of us who are part of this IPRG family today, as well as all those who will become part of it in the years to come. May He accompany us in our pursuit of knowledge and in our faithful dedication to our mission in this country of France.

May these fraternal gatherings continue to encourage and enrich us, guide and motivate us towards holistic growth, and help us to mature in our Christian faith through our day-to-day experiences in this foreign land.

As we celebrate this significant milestone, let us look back with gratitude, live the present with joy, and look towards the future with hope.

May the IPRG family continue to flourish, united in fraternity, strengthened by faith, enriched by diversity, and inspired by a common mission.

Viva our IPRG!

With prayerful wishes and fraternal affection,

Fr. Martin MARIA FRANCIS, SDB

President, IPRG
